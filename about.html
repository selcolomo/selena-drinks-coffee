A LITTLE ABOUT ME

Hi, my name is Selena Colomo and I am a third year at the University of Washington, studying Informatics on the Data Science track. Outside of school I enjoy running, hiking, going to concerts, reading, and spending time with my cat. And of course none of this would be possible without coffee to fuel me. I am originally from Albuquerque, New Mexico, but I absolutely love living in Seattle now (and ofc the endless supply of coffee shops is a contributing factor here).

My Coffee Favorites

I am a french press' number one supporter. Switching from morning Keurig coffee to my now french press coffee has set me on a better life trajectory path. And a morning coffee from my french press just would not be complete without one of my mugs from my much too large selection (the bigger the mug the better). But that's not what you're here to read about so let's talk coffee shops!

I can't tell you my favorite coffee shop right off the bat because that would ruin the fun, you're gonna have to look through my reviews to find that (and there might be a yearly coffee shop wrapped release at some point 🤫). So what do I look for in a good coffee shop? I want to say off the bat that although I consider myself a highly trained coffee connoisseur others might think of me as an amateur, so all reviews here are highly opinionated and subjective.

What I Look for in a Coffee Shop

1. Ambiance

This one is pretty important to me, because as a college student currently making minimum wage sometimes the only way I can justify an $8 coffee is if I at least get some homework done with it. Unfortunately for this and most of the categories I judge on, there isn't much of a strict rubric but more so just how I feel about it at the moment. I appreciate room to sit and a table to work, plenty of outlets, bathrooms, and water stations are a nice plus. I personally am not a fan of the super clean and modern look, but rather prefer some fun and whimsy.

2. Price

I live in Seattle so it's not unusual to spend way more than I should for one coffee that I'll probably finish in 10 minutes. But, I draw the line if I am paying an extra dollar for my almondmilk and every syrup. I'm sorry but $9 for a 12oz latte is a bit insane. Butttttt, if it's really really yummy sometimes I'll make an exception. I also don't usually opt to eat at coffee shops just because I'd rather not spend $14 on a tiny breakfast sandwich. The occasional croissant though is different.

3. Coffee

Last but not least the coffee itself. Again, I do not have a strict rubric for deciding what makes a good coffee, it's more so either I like it or don't. Some things I do look for in my coffee though are:

  • Strong coffee.
  • Not too milky. If I get my coffee back and the color is off, my mood will be ruined.
  • Not too sweet. Occasionally I will indulge in sweeter coffee, in which case it's fine, but if I order a basic lavender latte and it tastes like straight sugar, it's usually a turn off.
